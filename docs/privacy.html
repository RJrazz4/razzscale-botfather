Privacy Policy — RazzScale AI

Last updated: 25 October 2026

RazzScale AI ("RazzScale", "we", "us") provides WhatsApp-based digital services, including automated chat, music delivery, and bot-creation tools, through the WhatsApp Business Platform. This policy explains what information we process when you interact with our WhatsApp number or services, and how it is used.

Information we process

  • Phone number and profile name — provided by WhatsApp when you message us, used to identify your conversation and reply to you.
  • Message content — the messages you send to our number, processed only to provide the service you requested (for example, answering a query, delivering a song, or creating a bot).
  • Service records — basic logs such as timestamps, delivery status, and feature usage, kept for reliability, abuse prevention, and improving the service.

How we use information

  • To operate and respond within our WhatsApp services.
  • To maintain security, prevent spam and abuse, and fix technical problems.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your messages for advertising profiling.

Sharing

We use Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and cloud hosting providers strictly to run the service. Information is shared only as needed for message delivery and infrastructure operation, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Conversation data is retained only as long as needed to provide the service. You may ask us to delete your data at any time by messaging our WhatsApp number with the word "DELETE" or by emailing us; we will remove your stored conversation data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time by simply not messaging the number, or by blocking it. Blocking our number stops all further processing of new messages from you.
